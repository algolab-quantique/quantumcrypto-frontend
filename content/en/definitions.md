# Technical Definitions and Explanations

This file contains definitions and explanations for technical terms used throughout the platform.

## Key: definition.photon
### Photon {#photon}
A photon is a quantum of electromagnetic radiation and the fundamental particle of light. In quantum cryptography, photons are used as carriers of quantum information due to their quantum properties.

## Key: definition.encryption-key
### Encryption Key {#encryption-key}
An encryption key is a piece of information that determines the functional output of a cryptographic algorithm. In quantum cryptography, this key is generated through quantum mechanical processes that ensure its security.

## Key: definition.public-channel
### Public Channel {#public-channel}
A communication channel that can be monitored by anyone, including potential eavesdroppers. In quantum cryptography protocols, public channels are used for classical communication while quantum channels carry the quantum information.

## Key: definition.quantum-channel
### Quantum Channel {#quantum-channel}
A communication medium that can transmit quantum states, typically photons. Unlike classical channels, quantum channels can preserve quantum properties like superposition and entanglement.

## Key: definition.bit-encoding
### Bit Encoding {#bit-encoding}
The process of representing classical binary information (0s and 1s) using quantum states, typically the polarization states of photons in quantum cryptography protocols.

## Key: definition.orthogonal-states
### Orthogonal States {#orthogonal-states}
Two quantum states are orthogonal if they are completely distinguishable and cannot be confused with each other when measured. In BB84, bits are encoded using orthogonal polarization states.

## Key: definition.quantum-entanglement
### Quantum Entanglement {#quantum-entanglement}
A quantum phenomenon where two or more particles become correlated in such a way that the quantum state of each particle cannot be described independently, even when separated by large distances.

## Key: definition.basis
### Measurement Basis {#basis}
The set of quantum states used to measure a quantum system. In quantum cryptography, the choice of measurement basis affects what information can be extracted from a quantum state.

## Key: definition.eavesdropper
### Eavesdropper (Eve) {#eavesdropper}
In quantum cryptography, Eve represents a malicious third party attempting to intercept and gain information about the communication between Alice and Bob without being detected.
